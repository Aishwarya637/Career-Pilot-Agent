🚀 CareerPilot AI — Career Copilot Agent

Your AI-Powered Personal Career Assistant

📌 Overview

CareerPilot AI is an intelligent career assistance agent designed to simplify the job search process using Artificial Intelligence. It helps job seekers analyze their resumes, discover relevant job opportunities, and customize their resumes according to specific job descriptions.

Instead of manually reviewing resumes and searching for opportunities, users can leverage AI-powered assistance to make their job search more organized, targeted, and efficient.

The project combines resume analysis, job discovery, and AI-driven resume tailoring into one career-focused workflow.

✨ Key Features

📄 1. AI Resume Scanner

Analyzes uploaded resumes to understand a candidate's skills, qualifications, and experience.

Identifies relevant strengths and potential areas for improvement.

Helps users understand how their profile can align with career opportunities.

🔎 2. Job Search & Application Links

Helps users discover job opportunities relevant to their skills and profile.

Provides job listings and application links where available.

Makes it easier to navigate to relevant job postings and apply directly through the available links.

🎯 3. AI-Powered Resume Tailoring

Analyzes a specific job description.

Compares the job requirements with the candidate's resume.

Suggests relevant skills, keywords, and experience to emphasize.

Generates a tailored resume version aligned with the target role.

Helps improve the relevance of a resume for individual job applications.

Important: Resume tailoring should preserve the candidate's real qualifications and experience rather than inventing skills or achievements.

⚙️ How It Works

Upload Resume: The user provides their existing resume for analysis.

Analyze Profile: The agent extracts relevant information, including skills, qualifications, and experience.

Discover Opportunities: The system helps identify suitable job openings and provides available application links.

Enter Job Description: The user provides the job description for a specific target role.

Tailor Resume: The AI compares the resume against the job requirements and creates a targeted version.

Apply with Confidence: The user reviews the tailored resume and follows the relevant job application links.

💡 Problem Statement

Job seekers often face three major challenges:

Understanding whether their resume highlights the right skills for a role.

Finding relevant job openings without spending excessive time searching.

Customizing their resumes for different job descriptions.

CareerPilot AI aims to address these challenges through a unified, AI-assisted career workflow that helps users move from resume analysis to targeted job applications.
